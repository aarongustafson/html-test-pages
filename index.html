Accessibility test cases

A focused collection of representative test pages covering interaction, semantics, dynamic content, and visual presentation.

  • Keyboard and dragging

    Tests complex grid navigation, focus behavior, and keyboard alternatives to dragging.

  • Dialog and popover examples

    Exercises modal behavior, focus management, and native popovers.

  • Live region examples

    Demonstrates dynamic announcements, validation feedback, and status messages.

  • Info icon tooltips

    Tests content revealed by hover and focus, including its visibility and persistence.

  • Text contrast examples

    Covers static, opacity-based, and interactive-state contrast failures.
